# QR Code Strategy Guide

This guide helps you choose the best QR code strategy for your specific business needs and industry.

## Restaurant and Hospitality
For restaurants, cafes, and bars, we highly recommend using **Dynamic QR Codes** to link to a digital menu.
- **Why Dynamic?** You can update your menu items, prices, or seasonal specials without having to reprint the QR codes on your tables.
- **Placement Tips:** Place the QR codes on table tents, coasters, or directly on the table. If placing them on a patio or outdoor area, ensure you use weather-resistant materials or laminate them to prevent fading and water damage.
- **Size:** Ensure the QR code is at least 2x2 cm (0.8x0.8 inches) for easy scanning under dim restaurant lighting.

## Real Estate
Real estate agents can use QR codes on flyers, yard signs, and business cards to immediately engage potential buyers.
- **Best Use Case:** Link the QR code to a 3D virtual tour of the property, a video walkthrough, or a detailed image gallery.
- **Call to Action (CTA):** Always include a catchy CTA next to the QR code. For example: "Scan to take a 3D Virtual Tour!" or "See inside this home right now!" 
- **Tracking:** Use our analytics dashboard to track how many scans each yard sign receives to determine which neighborhoods are showing the most interest.

## Event Management (Tickets and RSVPs)
If you are hosting an event, QR codes can streamline the ticketing and RSVP process.
- **How to use:** Place a QR code on the invitation that links to an RSVP Google Form, Eventbrite page, or a custom landing page.
- **Engagement:** After the event, you can use a QR code on the exit signs linked to a feedback survey.
