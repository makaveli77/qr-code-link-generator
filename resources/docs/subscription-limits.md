# Subscription Limits and Plans

This document outlines the usage limits, analytics, and features available under each subscription plan.

## Free Tier (Basic)
Your starting point for creating basic QR codes to simple URLs.
- **Dynamic Links:** 3 dynamic links maximum.
- **Static Links:** Unlimited.
- **Scans:** Up to 500 total scans per month across all your dynamic codes combined.
- **Analytics:** Basic tracking available (Total scans and date).
- **Expiration:** Links will display an "expired limit reached" notice if the 500 scan limit is exceeded for the month. To lift the limit, you must upgrade your tier.

## Pro Tier
Designed for small businesses, real estate, and event promoters needing detailed tracking.
- **Dynamic Links:** Up to 100 dynamic links.
- **Static Links:** Unlimited.
- **Scans:** Up to 50,000 total scans per month.
- **Analytics:** Advanced tracking (Scans by device type, OS, geolocation by Country/City).
- **Custom Domains:** Connect your own domain for branded short links (e.g., link.yourbrand.com).
- **Expiration:** None, provided the account subscription is active.

## Enterprise Tier
Built for large-scale marketing campaigns, agencies, and massive volume.
- **Dynamic Links:** Unlimited.
- **Static Links:** Unlimited.
- **Scans:** Unlimited.
- **Analytics:** Real-time advanced dashboard with full CSV export capabilities.
- **API Access:** Full access to programmatically generate and manage links via our REST API.
- **Service & Support:** Priority email support and dedicated account manager.
